# Field office note: soil moisture sensor care

Capacitance soil moisture sensors drift as the soil around them settles and as salts
build up. Check each sensor against a hand sample (the feel and appearance method)
at the start of every season and after any heavy cultivation. Re-seat a sensor if its
readings jump by more than 5 percentage points in a day without rain or irrigation.
Keep cables away from tillage paths and replace a sensor if its readings stay flat
for more than two days.
